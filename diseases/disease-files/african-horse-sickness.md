**Specific Causes:** African Horse Sickness virus (AHSV; genus Orbivirus; 9 serotypes)

**Affects****:** Primarily horses (most susceptible); donkeys (carriers); zebras (resistant); dogs (fatal form after eating infected horse meat)

**Primary Organ System:** Respiratory; Cardiovascular

**Secondary Organ System:** Lymphoid

**Transmission:** Adult Culicoides midges (C. imicola and C. bolitinos); females feed on viraemic animals and transmit with next blood meal; active dusk to dawn; virogenesis in midge = 8 days

**Clinical Signs:**

Peracute:

- Peracute (pulmonary / dunkop): fever; severe dyspnoea; paroxysmal coughing; frothy serofibrinous discharge from nose; sudden death within hours; <5% recover; 95% mortality

- Subacute (cardiac / dikkop): subcutaneous oedema of head, neck and supraorbital fossa (prominent); petechiae in conjunctival mucosa, mouth and ventral mouth; paralysis of oesophagus; fever; colic; 50% mortality

Acute:

- Acute (mixed): respiratory distress and oedematous swelling; 70% mortality

- Horse sickness fever form (mild): occurs in immunised/partially immune horses and donkeys/zebras; slight fever; slightly laboured breathing; increased heart rate

**Pathological signs:**

- Respiratory (dunkop): severe oedema of lungs; hydrothorax; marked diffuse congestion of glandular stomach mucosa

- Cardiovascular (dikkop): severe hydropericardium; pale/greyish blanched areas in myocardium; mucosal petechiae

- Integumentary: yellowish gelatinous subcutaneous oedema of head, neck and supraorbital fossa

- Epi- and endocardial haemorrhages

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Multiplication in regional lymph nodes → primary viraemia → infection of lungs and lymphoid tissues → secondary viraemia → endothelial damage → effusions into body cavities; oedematous change in lung tissue; serosal and visceral haemorrhages

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Virus isolation (heparin tube — green)

- PCR (EDTA tube — purple)

- Serology (red/yellow tube)

- Lung and spleen on ice or in formalin for IHC

**Notifiable / Controlled Disease:** yes
