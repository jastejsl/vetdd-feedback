**Specific Causes:** Porcine circovirus type 2 (PCV-2; Circoviridae; smallest known animal virus; circular/ring DNA)

**Affects:** Pigs

Commonly affects pigs 2–3 weeks post-weaning (8–12 weeks of age)

**Primary Organ System:** Lymphoid/Immune; Respiratory

**Secondary Organ System:** Renal; Integumentary; Reproductive

**Clinical Signs:**

- Wasting and unthriftiness (post-weaning multisystemic wasting syndrome — PMWS)

- Dyspnoea

- Enlarged peripheral lymph nodes

- PDNS (Porcine Dermatitis and Nephropathy Syndrome): dark reddish skin lesions

Acute:

- Progressive wasting in weaned pigs

- Respiratory distress

- Lymphadenopathy

**Pathological signs:**

- Lymphoid: very enlarged spleen and lymph nodes; intracytoplasmic inclusion bodies; multinucleate giant cells

- Renal: swollen kidneys with white spots

- Respiratory: lungs rubbery, mottled, fail to collapse

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- PCV-2 infects immune cells → lymphoid depletion and immunosuppression → secondary infections and multisystemic wasting; PDNS: immune-complex deposition in skin and kidneys

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Three criteria: clinical wasting + lesions + presence of PCV-2 antigen/DNA

- PCR; histopathology with immunohistochemistry

**Lab Findings**:

- Intracytoplasmic inclusion bodies; multinucleate giant cells on histopathology
