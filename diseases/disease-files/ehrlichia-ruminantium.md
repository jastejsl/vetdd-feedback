**Common names:** Heartwater

**Affects:** Cattle; sheep; goats; some wild ruminants

**Primary Organ System:** Nervous system

**Secondary Organ System:** Cardiovascular; Haematological

**Identification tips:** 
(Biphasic life cycle)

- Elementary bodies (EB): small, 0.5–2.0 µm, extracellular infective forms. 

- Reticulate bodies (RB): larger, 0.75–2.5 µm, intracellular, non-infective. 

- Morulae (colonies) in cytoplasm of endothelial cells  - visible on Giemsa-stained brain smear.

**Clinical Signs:**

- Fever

- Neurological signs (from endothelial damage in CNS)

- Respiratory distress

- Death (very high mortality)

**Pathological signs:**

- Brain: Morulae of E. ruminantium in endothelial cells; oedema (heartwater)

- Pericardium/pleural cavity: Hydropericardium; hydrothorax; ascites

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Amblyomma tick feeds on acutely ill animal → EBs ingested → develop in tick gut cells + salivary glands → infective EBs transmitted to vertebrate host → infect vascular endothelial cells → binary fission (morulae) → cell lysis → oedema + neurological signs

**Diagnosis:**

- Pathology

- Giemsa-stained brain smear (morulae in endothelial cells)

- Serology (IFAT, ELISA)

- PCR

**Notifiable / Controlled Disease:** yes
