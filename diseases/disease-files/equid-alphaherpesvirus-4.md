**Specific Causes:** Equid alphaherpesvirus 4 

**A.K.A:** Equine Viral Rhinopneumonitis

**Affects:** Horses (narrow host range)

**Primary Organ System:** Respiratory

**Secondary Organ System:** Reproductive (rarely)

**Transmission:** Aerosol; fomites; direct and indirect contact during inhalation and ingestion; transplacental (possible)

**Clinical Signs:**

- Rhinopneumonia: sneezing; coughing; nasal discharge; conjunctivitis

- Biphasic fever

- Abortions (rare)

**Pathological signs:**

- Respiratory: rhinitis; necrosis of respiratory epithelium

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Virus proliferates in nasal mucosa → rhinitis, fever and necrosis of respiratory epithelium → rarely fatal

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Virus isolation (nasopharyngeal swabs)

- Serology: ICF, IFA, serum neutralisation
