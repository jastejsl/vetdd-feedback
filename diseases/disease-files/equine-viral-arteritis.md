**Specific Causes:** Equine arteritis virus (genus Equartevirus; Arteriviridae)

**A.K.A:** Pink Eye / Epizootic Cellulitis

**Affects:** Horses

South Africa is EVA-free; notifiable and controlled disease; carrier stallions shed virus in semen

**Primary Organ System:** Vascular; Respiratory

**Secondary Organ System:** Reproductive; Integumentary; Ocular

**Transmission:** Aerosols from respiratory tract; fomites; placentas/foetuses; carrier stallions shed in semen (not in urine or respiratory secretions)

**Clinical Signs:**

- Extremely variable clinical presentation

- High abortion rates

- Dyspnoea

- Subcutaneous oedema of head, legs, and eyelids

- Scrotal and preputial oedema

- Epiphora (excessive tearing)

- Severe lower limb oedema

**Pathological signs:**

- Vascular: pan-vasculitis → oedema, haemorrhage, and infarcts

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Initial replication in lung macrophages → spread to bronchial lymph nodes → circulatory system → pan-vasculitis → oedema, haemorrhage, and infarcts throughout body

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Virus isolation

- Serology (virus neutralisation)

- PCR

**Notifiable / Controlled Disease:** yes
