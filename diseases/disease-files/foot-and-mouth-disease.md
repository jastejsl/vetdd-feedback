**Specific Causes:** Foot-and-mouth disease virus (7 serotypes: A, O, C, SAT-1, SAT-2, SAT-3, Asia-1)

**Affects:** All cloven-hoofed animals

**Primary Organ System:** Integumentary; Oral/Digestive

**Secondary Organ System:** Cardiovascular (young animals); Reproductive; Mammary

**Transmission:** Primarily inhalation; oral (1000× more virus needed); pigs: oral route predominant; virus shed in all body secretions; saliva contains largest amount; direct and indirect (air, fomites)

**Clinical Signs:**

Acute:

- Fever; dullness; inappetence

- Salivation (excess)

- Lameness; disinclination to stand

- Vesicles on mouth, muzzle, feet and teats rupturing to leave ulcers

- Milk production drop

- Young animals: myocarditis (tiger-heart disease)

- Cows: lesions on udder leading to secondary mastitis

**Pathological signs:**

- Oral/integumentary: vesicular lesions at skin-hoof junction, oral mucosa, tongue; erosions fill with fibrin; separation of horn from underlying tissue

- Cardiac: myocarditis in young animals (tiger heart)

**Pathophysiology / Mechanism:**

- Initial replication in mucosa and lymphoid tissues of pharynx and soft palate → regional lymph node infection → viraemia → infection of squamous epithelium → bullae form (lysis of swollen cells releases intracellular fluid into intercellular spaces) → vesicles rupture → erosions → secondary bacterial infections

**Diagnosis:**

- Clinical Signs

- Pathology

- Virus isolation: epithelium covering vesicles

- Antigen detection: ELISA or PCR

- Serology: ELISA

- Tissue samples: epithelial tissue from vesicles; vesicular fluid; probang

**Notifiable / Controlled Disease:** yes
