# July build updates

July's build upgrades focussed on completing the scope of the feature-set needed to bring VetDD nearer to the end-state initially envisioned. The focus of it was on supporting a feature-complete image suite (presentation, categorisation, uploading) and on research (literature) integration.

Smaller changes apart from the above two aspects will also be covered.

## Image Suite

To ensure a holistic image suite is supported in VetDD, two main aspects needed to be covered:

1. Categorisation of each image batch to better group related images
2. The ability for a user to upload images to openly enrich the content in VetDD

Each has been briefly covered below.

### Categorisation of images

A case of Spirocerca lupi or Babesia canis will result in multiple clinical signs, pathological signs and have varying appearances of the disease-causing agent itself. As a result, three broad groups for image classification were created to group the associated images:

- Example of Agent
- Classic presentation
- PM Changes

The hope is that by allowing for images to be sorted according into any of the three above groups, during a recall/exploration of the app, the user is well-equipped to view only the information they desire. 

A simple "Show" button per group has been added in each entry where an image is available to display.

### Uploading images

If I were to rely on solely my exposure to the pathogens hosted in VetDD before I could upload an image for them (regardless of group), it would take an eternity before the app is populated with adequate information.

To expedite the process of acquiring the images for the various pathogens, I have thus built a system whereby any user can upload an image for a specific pathogen. A user can now upload an image and categorise it themselves. The hope is of course that this feature is utilised increasingly over time.

## Literature integration

This was by far the hardest part of July's build work. It has two components to it:

1. The in-app literature additions
2. A dedicated "Research" page

### In-app literature additions

A personal discontent of mine (which also formed part of my pursuit of creating this app) was the continuous referral to "the latest literature" by lecturers whenever discussion of a certain disease or other veterinary concept was brought up. Not that the literature is not important however, given the plethora of tasks that take up conducting one's self in a "normal" day, dedicating additional time to keep up with "the latest literature" in a certain field becomes an impossible ask. 

As a result, it was a desire of mine to have the literature 'brought to me' for each disease of relevance or interest…
This is essentially what has been built thus far in VetDD.

Now, per each entry, there is a "Explore literature" button that has been added which, upon clicking, presents the user with the latest literature surrounding that entry. It is limited by the data sources it is relying upon to populate the information however, this is something I do plan on expanding in the near future - should interest/demand for this pick up.

Segmentation into "Diagnose" and "Treatment" have also been provided to aid subsect the knowledge that is desired instead of needing to navigate a wormhole of literature however, these may be discontinued if results prove unreliable (awaiting feedback).

Included in this literature integration are two pre-configured AI (artificial intelligence) triggers that enable a user to have the article summarised for them or analysed for them without ever needing to open the paper itself!

The prompts behind the "Summarise" and "Analyse" triggers are something I can fine-tweak based on user feedback however, this method of providing AI tooling essentially empowers a user to utilise the technology with guardrails. The guardrails are ironically the steps any person using AI tooling should be taking on their own however, laziness prevails hence the hand-holding here.

Overall, progress in direction of the desired end-state to unify the components between "education", "academia" and "workmanship" in a veterinary field.

### Research page

Given the work that was already put in to power the main app with the literature additions, not much effort was required to create a "search engine" like page specifically for academic research purposes within VetDD. This is now publicly accessible (no account) at vetdd.org/research

Some nice features such as "Save" and "Hide" have been added for UX purposes when browsing such literature sites, along with commentary for both subsets customisable by the user if desired. 

AI tooling has also been enabled on this dedicated research page whereby a user can, based on the articles they save ONLY, compare or via custom-prompts, interact with AI to help them understand the articles. After a lone discussion with a Dr, I have a (slightly) better understanding of how students are required to analyse/dissect a research paper so, my hope is that I am able to create in-depth prompts on the backend that via a trigger button a user is able to simply receive the end-result of the analysis/AI interpretation.

The above two summate the main two changes that were made to bring the feature-set nearer to completion 

## Search bar enhancements

A minor change was also made to the search bar whereby a user can now directly activate/deactive a filter simply by searching for the filter's descriptor in the search bar. Example: typing "Ascites" will provide the user with the option to trigger "Ascites" as a Pathological Sign. 

It was found that some users may have been attempting to do this hence the addition of this feature.
